# Off-Label Drugs for Cancer Overview

This library covers repurposed and off-label drugs being explored in oncology.

### What this library includes

These topics are structured similarly to the natural-compound topics, but the focus is on:

* drug repurposing rationale
* mechanism of action
* evidence by cancer type
* dosing and scheduling patterns
* safety and interaction issues
* practical sourcing or access questions

### What this library is for

Use this library when you want a more structured view of off-label drugs in oncology, including what evidence exists, how the drugs are thought to work, and what the practical safety issues are.
