# Colorectal Cancer

Colorectal cancer is one of the more clinically useful areas for mebendazole because there is both mechanistic relevance and early biomarker-focused human data.

### Overview

The colorectal-cancer interest includes anti-angiogenic signalling and tolerated dosing in metastatic disease.

### Key findings

* In metastatic colorectal cancer, 500 mg twice daily produced significant VEGF reduction after 12 weeks in a tolerated-dose setting
* The anti-VEGF signal supports the broader angiogenesis-inhibition story
* Mebendazole remains investigational, but colorectal cancer gives it one of its better early human footholds

### Clinical positioning

This is an early but meaningful clinical signal, and one of the more practical repurposing pages in the topic.
