# Cancer Immunology Overview

This is the main hub for cancer-immunology topics on the site.

Use it to explore immune biology, treatment research, patient advocacy, and resources as they are added.

### Immunology vs immunotherapy <a href="#immunology-vs-immunotherapy" id="immunology-vs-immunotherapy"></a>

* Immunology is the broad study of the immune system: how immune cells recognise danger, maintain tolerance, and interact with tissues, infections, autoimmunity, and cancer.
* Cancer immunology focuses specifically on the dynamic interaction between the immune system and tumours, including surveillance, escape mechanisms, and the tumour-immune microenvironment.
* _Immunotherapy_ sits inside this bigger space: it’s any treatment that harnesses or modulates the immune system to treat disease, including cancer, allergies, autoimmune disease, infections, and more

The immune environment includes cells, tumour signals, inflammation, and tissue access. It can influence whether immunotherapy has a realistic chance to work.

* [T Cells in Cancer: A Patient Overview](t-cells-overview.md) explains cells that immunotherapies try to activate or direct.
* [Macrophages in Cancer](../macrophages-in-cancer/) covers immune cells that may support or suppress anti-tumour responses.
