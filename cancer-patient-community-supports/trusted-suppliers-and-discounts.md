#### Category 1: BCL-2/BCL-xL Anti-Apoptotic Pathway Inhibitors <a href="#category-1-bcl-2bcl-xl-anti-apoptotic-pathway-inhi" id="category-1-bcl-2bcl-xl-anti-apoptotic-pathway-inhi"></a>

**Mechanism:** These are the best-validated natural senolytics in current research. Senescent cells over-express BCL-2 family proteins (BCL-2, BCL-xL, BCL-W) to resist apoptosis — these compounds block those survival shields and force dormant cancer cells into apoptosis.

**Fisetin (liposomal, 150 mg) ⭐ Highest priority**

The most extensively validated natural senolytic — comparable in animal studies to pharmaceutical agents like navitoclax. Selectively induces apoptosis in senescent cells via BCL-xL inhibition. Currently in Phase II clinical trial specifically for breast cancer survivors post-chemotherapy to address TIS.

_Available as:_ [_Fisetin Pro Liposomal_ ](https://www.mcsformulas.com/vitamins-supplements/fisetin-pro-liposomal/ref/14)_at MCS Formulas_

**Quercetin (with Bromelain, 400 mg) ⭐ Core senolytic**

One of the first identified natural senolytics (the D+Q combination). Acts on multiple BCL-2 family pro-survival pathways in senescent cells; also well-studied combined with dasatinib in research settings. Bromelain adds anti-inflammatory synergy.

_Available as:_ [_Quercetin & Bromelain_](https://www.mcsformulas.com/vitamins-supplements/quercetin-bromelain-400-mg-100-caps/ref/14) _at MCS Formulas_

**Apigenin (liposomal, 200 mg)**

Upregulates pro-apoptotic Bax and downregulates BCL-2/Mcl-1 in cancer cells, shifting the balance toward apoptosis. Synergises with BCL-2 inhibitors (ABT-263 class) in colorectal cancer models — effectively amplifying their senolytic action.

_Available as:_ [_Apigenin Pro Liposomal_](https://www.mcsformulas.com/vitamins-supplements/apigenin-pro-liposomal/ref/14) _at MCS Formulas_

**Luteolin (liposomal, 150 mg)**

A flavone closely related to apigenin with overlapping BCL-2 family modulation and SASP suppression. Acts on NF-κB (a SASP driver) and induces mitochondria-dependent apoptosis in senescent/cancer cells.

_Available as:_ [_Luteolin Pro Liposomal_](https://www.mcsformulas.com/vitamins-supplements/luteolin-pro-liposomal/ref/14) _at MCS Formulas_

#### Category 2: ROS-Mediated Senolytics — Oxidative Stress Exploitation <a href="#category-2-ros-mediated-senolytics--oxidative-stre" id="category-2-ros-mediated-senolytics--oxidative-stre"></a>

**Mechanism:** Senescent cancer cells often have elevated baseline ROS and depleted antioxidant reserves. These compounds exploit that vulnerability by further raising ROS to lethal levels — a threshold that normal cells don't cross.

**Piperlongumine (liposomal, 40 mg) ⭐ Core senolytic**

One of the original validated senolytics. Kills senescent cells primarily through ROS elevation and glutathione depletion. The effect is reversed by NAC, confirming the ROS mechanism as central. Liposomal form is preferred for bioavailability.

_Available as:_ [_Piperlongumine Pro Liposomal_](https://www.mcsformulas.com/vitamins-supplements/piperlongumine-liposomal/ref/14) _at MCS Formulas_

**Piperlongumine (whole herb source, 500 mg)**

The natural whole-herb source of piperlongumine — a budget alternative or adjunct to the concentrated liposomal form. Lower active compound concentration.

_Available as:_ [_Long Pepper_](https://www.mcsformulas.com/en/vitamins-supplements/piperlogumine/ref/14) _at MCS Formulas_

**Baicalein (liposomal, 200 mg)**

Induces apoptosis in colon cancer cells via MAPK ERK/p38 signalling, and separately drives cancer cell senescence via the baicalin/telomerase route. The dual apoptotic + senescence-induction profile is unique in this category.

_Available as:_ [_Baicalein Pro Liposomal_](https://www.mcsformulas.com/vitamins-supplements/baicalein-pro-liposomal/ref/14) _at MCS Formulas_

#### Understanding Senolytics in Cancer Dormancy Management <a href="#understanding-senolytics-in-cancer-dormancy-manage" id="understanding-senolytics-in-cancer-dormancy-manage"></a>

_This is educational content which represents current peer-reviewed research on senolytic compounds and their mechanisms. This information is provided for educational purposes and does not constitute medical advice. Product availability and formulations are listed for reference only._

**What "Senolytic" Means in a Cancer Dormancy Context**

After therapy, a population of cancer cells can enter **therapy-induced senescence (TIS)** — a dormant, non-dividing state that is not dead. These cells survive via upregulated pro-survival (anti-apoptotic) pathways and secrete a **SASP (Senescence-Associated Secretory Phenotype)** — a cocktail of inflammatory cytokines and growth factors that if left unchecked can remodel the tumour microenvironment, fuel inflammation, and eventually allow dormant cells to re-emerge. **A senolytic stack pulsed periodically** selectively eliminates these senescent or dormant cells, while **senomorphics** dampen the SASP without killing the cells. Both have roles in a cancer dormancy post-treatment management strategy.\
\
See the [Senescence Resource Hub](../breast-cancer/er-positive-her2-negative/endocrine-therapy-resistance-and-dormancy/autophagy-and-senescence-in-antiestrogen-resistance/senescence-the-second-escape-route.md) for a deeper dive into this topic.
