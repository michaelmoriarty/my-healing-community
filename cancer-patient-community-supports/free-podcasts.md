# Free Podcasts

This page collects free podcast resources that may be useful for patients, carers, and readers exploring integrative oncology, healing, recovery, and practical support.

{% columns %}
{% column %}
### Radical Remission Project — Stories That Heal

This podcast shares powerful accounts from people who have experienced radical remission, alongside insights from the healers and doctors who supported them.

It is especially useful for readers looking for:

* real-life recovery stories
* hope and encouragement after diagnosis
* interviews that explore healing beyond conventional treatment alone

[Listen on Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/au/podcast/radical-remission-project-stories-that-heal-podcast/id1711453890)
{% endcolumn %}

### Flourish After Cancer

Hosted by **Dr Megan Bernard**, this podcast focuses on how to not just survive, but thrive after cancer.

It is especially useful for readers looking for:

* naturopathic and lifestyle-based recovery support
* research-backed nutrition and supplement discussion
* testing and survivorship strategies

[Listen on Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/the-flourish-after-cancer-podcast/id1818501250)

### The Yes to Life Show (UK)

This UK-based show focuses on cancer and asks deeper questions about what can be done better in treatment, support, and healing.

It is especially useful for readers looking for:

* broader cancer conversations
* integrative and supportive perspectives
* interviews and reflective discussion about cancer care

[Listen on Yes to Life](https://yestolife.org.uk/radio-shows/)

### The Healing Blueprint

Hosted by **Patricia**, this podcast grew out of a personal healing journey and focuses on practical healing strategies rather than on luck or passivity.

It is especially useful for readers looking for:

* simple lifestyle shifts
* foundational healing principles
* encouragement around restoring balance and resilience

[Listen on Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/show/4WULDQWKbwW3I5qCeIEH0o)
