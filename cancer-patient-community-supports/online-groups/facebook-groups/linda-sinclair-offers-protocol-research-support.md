### Linda's background

Linda’s first husband was diagnosed with inoperable, incurable stage 4 pancreatic cancer in April 2016.

With nothing to lose, he explored alternatives alongside conventional treatment, including cannabis oil.

Linda says he had a strong response.

His primary tumour was no longer detectable, and his metastases were greatly reduced.

He died from pneumonia in August 2017.

After that loss, Linda focused deeply on integrative oncology, naturopathic oncology, and the metabolic approach to cancer.

She has continued researching and sharing useful findings ever since.

Linda soon felt called to support patients with their own protocol research.

She saw this work as a way to honour her late husband.

In 2019, Linda remarried and created **The Always Hope Cancer Protocol Support Group** on Facebook.
