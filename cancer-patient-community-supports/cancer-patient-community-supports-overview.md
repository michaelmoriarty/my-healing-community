# Cancer Patient Community Supports Overview

This library brings together community-based support resources for people living with cancer, their families, and the wider healing community.

It is designed as a practical support space rather than a research-only space.

### What you'll find here

* free podcasts to check out
* books and reading recommendations
* online groups
* Facebook groups
* curated support resources and external links

### When to use this section

Use this library when you are looking for support, connection, shared learning, and practical community resources that apply across cancer types.

Breast-cancer-specific pathway tools and subtype-linked downloads now live inside the [Breast Cancer](https://app.gitbook.com/s/Iyy2bZWxLPaSj5B4dS3v/breast-cancer) section.
