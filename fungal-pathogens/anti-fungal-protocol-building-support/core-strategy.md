# Core Strategy

The core strategy treats the protocol like a coordinated team.

Each component has a distinct job.

### Examples of some strong team players

#### Laminarin

**Primary job:** occupy Dectin-1 without strongly activating it.

**Main benefit:** calms chronic inflammatory signalling and reduces oxidative stress, so cells can repair more effectively.

#### Usnea tincture

**Primary job:** interfere with fungal 14-3-3 proteins and virulence systems.

**Main benefit:** weaken mechanisms of fungal survival, invasion, and persistence.

#### β-Glucanase enzymes

**Primary job:** digest the β-glucan-rich biofilm matrix.

**Main benefit:** expose the fungal target and improve penetration of later steps.

#### Antifungal medication

**Primary job:** block the fungal ergosterol pathway.

**Main benefit:** direct kill or growth arrest.

#### Binders

**Primary job:** capture released toxins and debris.

**Main benefit:** reduce die-off burden and improve elimination.

### How the layers work together

* Laminarin turns down the alarm so tissues can shift out of crisis mode.
* Usnea weakens fungal defences.
* β-glucanase strips away the biofilm fortress.
* Antifungal medication hits a more exposed target.
* Binders mop up debris released during cleanup.

### Expected result

The intended outcome is:

* faster clearance
* less inflammation
* better tolerance
* quicker recovery
* better tissue healing

### Why the sequence matters

The protocol is not built around direct kill alone.

It is built around preparation, access, pressure, cleanup, and recovery.
