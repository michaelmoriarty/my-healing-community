# Expected Timeline & What to Watch For

This page maps the expected rhythm of the protocol.

### Week 1–2: Initial response

**What may be happening**

* biofilm begins to loosen
* first fungal die-off wave begins
* immune signalling starts to recalibrate

**What you may feel**

* fatigue
* headache
* digestive changes
* fluctuating energy

**What to do**

* increase binders and hydration
* slow the protocol if symptoms become too strong
* keep perspective if symptoms briefly worsen before improving

### Week 3–4: Turning point

**What may be happening**

* Dectin-1 danger signalling is more dampened
* fungal burden may be falling
* cells begin to shift from survival mode toward repair mode

**What you may feel**

* less die-off
* improving energy
* clearer thinking
* better sleep
* more stable digestion

**What to do**

* stay consistent
* get scheduled labs
* review progress with your practitioner

### Week 5: Rest week

This phase pauses laminarin and enzymes.

The aim is to give the body a short reset while antifungal pressure and binder support continue as advised.

**What to watch**

* stabilisation of gains
* less active die-off
* clearer view of your baseline without the full stack

### Week 6 onward: Continued progress or maintenance

Some people need only 4–8 weeks.

Others with deeper or more chronic burden may need 3–6 months.

**Common later changes**

* better energy
* clearer thinking
* less inflammatory reactivity
* better stress resilience
* more stable digestion

### Why timelines differ

Response varies with:

* severity and duration of burden
* overall health status
* genetics
* adherence
* ongoing mould exposure
* stress and lifestyle load

### Bottom line

Trust the process, but do not force it.

Track symptoms, keep your practitioner updated, and adjust pace when needed.
