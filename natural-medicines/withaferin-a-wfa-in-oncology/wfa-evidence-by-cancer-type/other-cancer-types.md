# Other Cancer Types

WFA has preclinical evidence across several additional cancer types. These settings do not yet support the same level of confidence as breast or ovarian cancer, but they are not trivial.

### Overview

| Cancer type     | Key signal                                                          | Evidence level                 |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Colorectal      | `HSP90`, `HIF-1α`, and EMT axis inhibition                          | Cell line and animal           |
| Osteosarcoma    | Published Phase I oncology trial and pro-apoptotic preclinical work | Phase I human plus preclinical |
| Prostate        | G2/M arrest and proteomic target mapping                            | Cell line established          |
| Pancreatic      | Oxaliplatin synergy and CSC-related signalling                      | Cell line only                 |
| Cervical        | HPV `E6/E7` downregulation and xenograft activity                   | Cell line and animal           |
| B-cell lymphoma | `NF-κB`, BCR signalling, and HSP90 disruption                       | Cell line and animal           |
| Hepatocellular  | Ferroptosis and resistance relevance via `Keap1/Nrf2`               | Cell line and animal           |
| Thyroid         | Sorafenib synergy                                                   | Cell line and animal           |

### What this means in practice

These settings expand the plausibility of WFA as a broad anticancer platform. They do not carry the same translational weight.

Osteosarcoma is the main exception because it has direct human Phase I relevance, even though efficacy remains unproven.

The others remain exploratory disease areas where mechanism is ahead of clinical translation.

### Bottom line

These thinner signals matter most as evidence of breadth, not as proof of near-term clinical readiness.
