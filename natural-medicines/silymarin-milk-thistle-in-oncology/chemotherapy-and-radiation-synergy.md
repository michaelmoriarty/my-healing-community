# Chemotherapy & Radiation Synergy

All synergy findings on this page refer to **Silymarin / Silibinin**, the active extract of **Milk Thistle**.

### Overview

One of Milk Thistle / Silymarin's most interesting oncology properties is its possible sensitiser-protector profile. In other words, it may enhance conventional treatment activity in some settings while also reducing treatment toxicity in healthy tissues.

### Synergy with doxorubicin

* Synergistic or additive effects reported in breast and colorectal cancer models
* Scheduling appears to matter in some colon carcinoma work
* Effects include enhanced apoptosis and altered drug-response behaviour

### Synergy with cisplatin and carboplatin

* Additive or enhanced antiproliferative effects reported in breast and gynaecological cancer models
* Stronger apoptotic responses seen in selected breast cancer settings

### Synergy with paclitaxel

* Low-dose pre-treatment synergy reported in standard colon carcinoma models
* Silymarin appears to influence drug uptake and cell-cycle effects in addition to growth inhibition

### Radiosensitisation

* Reviewed as a meaningful emerging area
* Reported mechanisms include higher oxidative stress inside tumour cells, greater DNA damage, and enhanced apoptosis after irradiation
* More clinical work is still needed before firm treatment protocols can be defined

### Hepatoprotection during chemotherapy

* Clinical studies suggest reduced AST and ALT elevation in some breast cancer patients receiving chemotherapy
* This may help support treatment continuity where liver toxicity would otherwise limit therapy
