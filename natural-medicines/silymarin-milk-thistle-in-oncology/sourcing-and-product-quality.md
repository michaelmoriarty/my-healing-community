# Sourcing & Product Quality

Silymarin is the bioactive extract of **Milk Thistle** (_Silybum marianum_). Product quality matters because the percentage of standardised **silymarin** directly changes how much active compound a reader actually gets.

### Why standardisation matters

Milk Thistle supplements vary enormously.

Raw seed powder may contain only a small percentage of silymarin, while a well-standardised extract provides far more active compound per capsule.

When comparing products, look for:

1. the **silymarin percentage**, not just the extract weight
2. clear source and extraction information
3. minimal fillers and unnecessary additives
4. capsule format and overall formulation quality

### Trusted product

The trusted option is the MCS Formulas Milk Thistle Silymarin 500 mg product.

At 500 mg extract standardised to 80% silymarin, each capsule provides roughly **400 mg of silymarin**.

That makes it a high-strength, standardised extract option compared with many general Milk Thistle powder products.

### Bioavailability and strength vary greatly

Milk Thistle supplements are not equal. A product may look similar on the front label but contain much less active silymarin in practice.

The most useful comparison points are:

* How many milligrams of extract does each capsule contains
* How many milligrams of extract does each capsule contain
* What percentage is standardised to silymarin
* How much active silymarin does this work out to per capsule
* Whether the product avoids unnecessary fillers

### Practical use notes

Based on the pharmacokinetic literature, absorption may be improved by:

* taking with a small amount of fat
* dividing doses across the day
* considering piperine or other bioenhancement strategies only with clinician awareness
