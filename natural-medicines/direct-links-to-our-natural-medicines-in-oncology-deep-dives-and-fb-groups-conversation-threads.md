# Direct links to our natural medicines in oncology deep dives and FB group's conversation threads

### This library brings together two things:

* Abbey's structured evidence grounded deep dives on individual natural compounds and selected molecules
* The Healing Cancer Study Support Group's A–Z archive of natural-compound and molecule discussion threads and file links

It also includes practical delivery guidance where liposomal formulation changes use, absorption, and safety.

#### How to use this section

1. Start with the compound or molecule deep dive if it already exists. More are being added each week.
2. Use the A–Z thread list below to read and comment within the private community discussion in the [Healing Cancer Study Support Group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/healingcancerstudysupport)
3. Use both together when you want structured evidence plus practical group context.

### What each deep dive includes

Each compound deep dive follows a shared core structure, but many now go further.

Most hubs include:

* overview
* evidence summary
* evidence by cancer type
* mechanisms
* safety and interactions
* dosing and timing
* sourcing and formulation quality

Many also include:

* pharmacokinetics and metabolism
* immune effects
* antimicrobial or antifungal relevance
* comparison or synergy pages
* more focused mechanism pages such as redox, NRF2, or ferroptosis

Some also include delivery-focused guidance where empty liposomes, liposomal melatonin, liposomal ivermectin, and safe use need their own pages.

Use this library of herbs, natural compounds, and selected molecules when you want an evidence-based view of what something is, how it works, where the evidence is strongest, and which practical safety, dosing, or treatment questions matter most.
