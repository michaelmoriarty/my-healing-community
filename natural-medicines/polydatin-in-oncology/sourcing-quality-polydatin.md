### Bioavailability and strength vary greatly

Bioavailability and potency can vary widely among polydatin supplements. One of the most important details is the amount of **actual polydatin per capsule** rather than the total capsule weight or the marketing language on the label.

Standard polydatin products are limited by poor absorption and rapid metabolism. Enhanced-delivery forms can perform much better, but they are not all equal. A product may appear cheaper at first glance, yet still offer lower real-world value if it delivers less active compound or uses a weaker delivery system.

This is why it helps to look at three things together:

* The amount of actual polydatin per capsule
* The delivery system used
* The price per bottle relative to the amount of active compound provided

When those factors are considered together, a stronger pro-liposomal formulation can work out better value over time than a cheaper-looking standard product.
