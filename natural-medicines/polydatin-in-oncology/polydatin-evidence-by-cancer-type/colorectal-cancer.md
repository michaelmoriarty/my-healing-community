# Colorectal Cancer

Polydatin is a glucoside precursor of resveratrol and has some of its strongest oncology relevance in colorectal cancer.

### Overview

The colorectal cancer literature includes radiosensitisation, chemosensitisation, and several detailed mechanistic studies.

### Key findings

* Combined polydatin and radiation reduced tumour number and volume in mouse models
* Polydatin enhanced oxaliplatin-induced cell death through the NOX5-ROS pathway
* It induced mitochondrial dysfunction, ER stress, and autophagosome formation in colon cancer cells
* Additional work suggests effects on PD-L1-related signalling, differentiation, and metastasis biology

### Practical interpretation

Colorectal cancer is one of the clearest examples of polydatin working as a mechanistically broad adjunct rather than a single-pathway compound.
