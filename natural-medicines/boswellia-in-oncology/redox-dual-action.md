# Redox Dual Action

Boswellia's redox behaviour is different from more classical dual-action compounds such as curcumin. Its dominant role is anti-inflammatory, but downstream oxidative stress can still contribute to tumour-cell kill in the right context.

### In healthy tissue

* suppresses NF-κB, COX-2, and 5-LOX
* reduces inflammatory damage
* supports normal-tissue resilience during radiotherapy or treatment stress

### In tumour cells

* downstream ROS accumulation may occur after PI3K/Akt inhibition and mitochondrial stress
* tumour cells dependent on inflammatory survival signalling may be more vulnerable to this shift

### Why 5-LOX matters

One of AKBA's distinctive features is **direct 5-lipoxygenase inhibition**. That gives Boswellia a unique anti-inflammatory angle compared with several other oncology adjuncts.

### Practical interpretation

Boswellia is best understood as primarily anti-inflammatory with a secondary, context-dependent oxidative component in tumour cells.
