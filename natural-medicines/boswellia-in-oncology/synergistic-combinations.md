# Synergistic Combinations

Boswellia's combination value is one of its strongest oncology features.

### With chemotherapy and radiotherapy

Common areas of interest include:

* cisplatin in NSCLC
* docetaxel resistance in prostate cancer
* taxane resistance in ovarian cancer
* radiosensitisation in glioblastoma and NSCLC
* protection against radiation-related cerebral oedema in brain tumour patients

### With natural compounds

Common pairings include:

* **Curcumin** — complementary NF-κB and inflammatory-pathway inhibition
* **Polydatin / resveratrol** — broader pathway coverage in integrative neuro-oncology logic
* **Piperine or Piper longum** — bioavailability support
* **Quercetin** — complementary inflammatory and signalling effects
* **Vitamin D3** — possible immune and differentiation overlap

### Practical interpretation

The curcumin-plus-Boswellia combination is especially compelling because it deepens inflammatory-pathway coverage without relying on identical mechanism profiles.
