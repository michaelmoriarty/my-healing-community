# Breast Cancer

Boswellia has one of its most important early human cancer datasets in **breast cancer**, which makes this a key page in the whole topic.

### Overview

The breast cancer evidence includes a 2024 Phase Ia human trial plus substantial preclinical work on AKBA, EGFR, apoptosis, and metastasis-related biology.

### Key human data

* A 2024 window-of-opportunity Phase Ia trial reported a statistically significant reduction in tumour proliferation marker Ki67 with oral _Boswellia serrata_
* Safety and tolerability were good in this early clinical setting
* This is one of the stronger direct human-tissue signals for any Boswellia oncology use

### Key preclinical data

* AKBA showed strong cytotoxic, apoptotic, and anti-metastatic activity in breast cancer cell lines
* EGFR phosphorylation and downstream PI3K/Akt signalling were inhibited in relevant models
* Comparative work suggests AKBA and ABA may offer complementary biology in breast cancer settings

### Clinical positioning

Breast cancer is one of the more important Boswellia pages because it combines real human data with strong mechanistic support.
