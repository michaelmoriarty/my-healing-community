# Ovarian Cancer

Boswellia has meaningful preclinical interest in **ovarian cancer**, especially around taxane resistance and multidrug-resistance biology.

### Overview

The strongest theme is AKBA's ability to reduce malignancy in taxane-resistant ovarian cancer through effects on drug-resistance machinery.

### Key preclinical data

* AKBA decreased malignancy in taxol-resistant ovarian cancer models
* Reduced P-glycoprotein and LRP-related multidrug-resistance expression
* Induced apoptosis in ovarian cancer cell lines
* Activity in 3D aggregate models supports relevance to metastatic behaviour

### Clinical positioning

This remains a preclinical but valuable area, especially because treatment resistance is such a dominant problem in recurrent ovarian cancer.
