# Colorectal Cancer

Boswellia has strong preclinical relevance in colorectal cancer, especially around inflammation, epigenetic effects, and metastasis biology.

### Overview

The colorectal cancer literature includes in vitro and orthotopic animal-model work with meaningful mechanistic depth.

### Key preclinical data

* AKBA inhibits growth, colony formation, migration, and invasion in colorectal cancer cells
* It upregulates tumour-suppressive miRNA families such as let-7 and miR-200
* Orthotopic mouse work confirms tumour suppression in vivo
* Boswellic acid also shows relevant Bax/Bcl-2 effects in colorectal cells
* mPGES-1 and inflammatory-pathway suppression add to the mechanistic rationale

### Clinical positioning

This remains a preclinical area, but one with good biological coherence and several distinct mechanisms beyond simple anti-inflammatory action.
