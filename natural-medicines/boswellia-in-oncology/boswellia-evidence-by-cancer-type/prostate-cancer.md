# Prostate Cancer

Boswellia has one of its strongest treatment-resistance stories in **prostate cancer**, especially in docetaxel-resistant disease models.

### Overview

The key theme here is resistance reversal through suppression of Akt and STAT3 survival signalling.

### Key preclinical data

* AKBA suppresses docetaxel-resistant prostate cancer in vitro and in vivo
* Reduces phospho-Akt and phospho-STAT3 in resistant tumours
* Shows activity in androgen-sensitive and androgen-independent models
* Orthotopic prostate cancer work supports genuine in vivo tumour suppression

### Clinical positioning

This is still preclinical, but clinically meaningful because docetaxel resistance is a major unmet need.
