# Pancreatic Cancer

Pancreatic cancer is one of the more compelling Boswellia settings from a preclinical standpoint because orthotopic model data exists.

### Overview

The strongest pancreatic data centres on growth suppression, metastasis reduction, and COX-2, NF-κB, MMP-9, and VEGF modulation in vivo.

### Key preclinical data

* Orthotopic nude-mouse work showed suppression of pancreatic tumour growth and metastasis
* AKBA downregulated COX-2, VEGF, MMP-9, and NF-κB-related signalling in tumour tissue
* These findings give Boswellia more credibility here than a purely cell-line-only evidence base would

### Clinical positioning

This remains a preclinical area, but an unusually meaningful one because pancreatic cancer is so hard to treat and orthotopic evidence is more translationally useful than simple cell-line work alone.
