# Non-Small Cell Lung Cancer

Boswellia has strong preclinical relevance in **non-small cell lung cancer**, with two especially important themes: **cisplatin sensitisation** and **radioresistance reversal**.

### Overview

The NSCLC literature includes cell-cycle arrest, apoptosis, autophagy suppression, platinum sensitisation, and epigenetic effects on radiation resistance.

### Key preclinical data

* AKBA causes G1/G0 arrest and apoptosis in NSCLC cells
* Suppresses autophagy survival pathways
* Sensitises NSCLC to cisplatin in preclinical work
* Reverses radiotherapy resistance via maspin methylation and AKT/FOXO1/p21-axis regulation

### Clinical positioning

This is still preclinical, but it is one of the more clinically interesting Boswellia areas because both platinum resistance and radioresistance matter in real treatment settings.
