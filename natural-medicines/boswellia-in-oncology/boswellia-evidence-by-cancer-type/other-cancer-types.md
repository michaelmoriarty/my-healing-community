# Other Cancer Types

Boswellia's strongest pages are glioblastoma, breast, colorectal, pancreatic, prostate, NSCLC, and ovarian cancer. Beyond those, several additional areas show promising but earlier-stage relevance.

### Additional evidence areas

* **Hepatocellular and gastric cancer** — G2/M arrest and Akt-related suppression have been reported
* **Haematologic malignancies** — preliminary apoptosis and NF-κB-related effects
* **Melanoma and fibrosarcoma** — pro-apoptotic and antiproliferative findings in preclinical work

### Practical interpretation

These are not the lead clinical pages for Boswellia, but they expand the biological range of the compound and may become more important as the literature grows.
