# Urolithin A Evidence by Cancer Type

This section groups the Urolithin A oncology evidence into focused cancer-type pages as the evidence base becomes strong enough to support them properly.

That structure keeps each cancer page easier to navigate and makes future updates simpler.

### Available pages

* [**Colorectal Cancer**](colorectal-cancer.md) — strongest current evidence, including Wnt signalling, AKT1-related immune effects, and chemotherapy sensitisation
* [**Pancreatic Cancer**](pancreatic-cancer.md) — one of the strongest mechanistic settings, with KRAS-pathway relevance, stromal remodelling, and anti-PD-1 sensitisation data
* [**Prostate Cancer**](prostate-cancer.md) — most clinically advanced evidence, including androgen-receptor signalling, NK-cell data, and the active phase II trial
* [**Breast Cancer**](breast-cancer.md) — subtype-based evidence across ER-positive, triple-negative, and HER2-positive contexts
* [**Endometrial Cancer**](endometrial-cancer.md) — ERα and ERβ remodelling, G2/M arrest, and one of the cleaner hormone-biology settings for Urolithin A
* [**Glioblastoma**](glioblastoma.md) — blood-brain-barrier penetration, Sirt1-FOXO1 signalling, and tumour-microenvironment findings
* [**Liver Cancer (HCC)**](liver-cancer-hcc.md) — direct HCC cell-line findings plus wider hepatoprotective and gut-liver-axis relevance in chronic liver disease
* [**Lung Cancer**](lung-cancer.md) — multiple independent NSCLC mechanisms, including EMT inhibition, autophagy-linked mTOR suppression, and in vivo xenograft evidence
* [**Osteosarcoma**](osteosarcoma.md) — early anti-metastatic evidence focused on migration, invasion, and MMP2/MMP9 suppression
* [**Bladder Cancer**](bladder-cancer.md) — unusually strong UMUC3 sensitivity, G2/M arrest, apoptosis, and plausible urinary-exposure relevance

If a cancer type is not listed here yet, it can be added later as the evidence base grows.
