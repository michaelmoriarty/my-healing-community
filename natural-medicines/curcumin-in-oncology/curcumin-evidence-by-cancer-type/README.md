# Curcumin Evidence by Cancer Type

This section groups the curcumin oncology evidence into individual cancer-type pages.

That structure keeps each cancer page shorter, keeps references next to the relevant discussion, and makes it easier to expand the topic over time.

### Included cancer pages

* Colorectal Cancer
* Breast Cancer
* Pancreatic Cancer
* Lung Cancer
* Prostate Cancer
* Head & Neck Cancer
* Gastric Cancer
* Blood Cancers

### Why this structure works better

* each cancer type can carry its own references
* readers can go straight to the cancer most relevant to them
* updates become easier as new studies appear
