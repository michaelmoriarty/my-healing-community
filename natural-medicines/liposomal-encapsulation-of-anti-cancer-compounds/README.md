# Liposomal Encapsulation of Anti-cancer Compounds

This section is educational only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Empty liposomes, DIY liposomal preparations, and experimental delivery methods can materially change absorption, tissue exposure, and side-effect risk.

This section explains why empty liposomes matter, how to use them, and where safety matters most.

It also includes focused guides on liposomal melatonin and liposomal ivermectin.

### How this section is organised

The heavier detail has been split into subpages.

Member reports are kept clearly separate from evidence summaries.

That is intentional.

It preserves the community voice without blending it into formal claims.
