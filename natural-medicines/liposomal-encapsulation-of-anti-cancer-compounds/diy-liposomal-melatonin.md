# DIY Liposomal Melatonin

Using empty liposomes plus melatonin powder to create a more bioavailable melatonin mix at far lower cost than most ready-made liposomal products.

{% hint style="warning" %}
This page is educational only. It is not a prescription. Liposomal delivery may materially change melatonin exposure compared with ordinary oral powder or capsules.
{% endhint %}

### What the research suggests

Liposomal or encapsulated melatonin may increase blood levels by roughly **2–3 times**, sometimes more, compared with standard oral powder.

That does **not** automatically mean outcomes improve in the same proportion.

It does mean that equal milligram amounts may no longer equate to equal exposure.

### Intended daily dose

The intended daily dose is not a suggestion simply an example to guide ratios:

* **180 mg/day**

{% hint style="info" %}
Want to understand how much of a **180 mg liposomal dose** may actually be reaching circulation? See [Dosing, Bioavailability, and Human Scaling](../melatonin-in-oncology-study-notes/dosing-bioavailability-and-human-scaling.md).
{% endhint %}

#### Working dose notes:

* **0.9 mL** of the finished mixture provides **180 mg**
* a small syringe with **0.1 mL markings** gives better precision
* the bottle should be gently shaken before drawing each dose
* the syringe should be rinsed after use
* if 60mg liposomal per day is the target, then the syringe with 180mg could last 3 days
* store the syringe in a clean storage box in the fridge on its side if using the 180mg across more than one day.

### Why protect melatonin from light

Melatonin is light-sensitive.

Even liposomal encapsulation does **not** fully remove the risk of photodegradation.

That is why careful storage matters:

* amber or opaque containers
* fridge storage
* minimising light exposure

### Why use liposomes here

The reasoning is straightforward:

* standard oral melatonin has limited bioavailability
* much is lost through digestion and first-pass metabolism
* liposomal delivery may increase the amount reaching the bloodstream

Many preclinical cancer studies used injection routes rather than routine oral delivery.

The DIY liposomal approach is one way to move closer to higher-exposure conditions **without needles**.

### Making 3 weeks worth at 180mg per day

<details>

<summary>Quick guide to measurements</summary>

* **20 mL** of Liposence
* **4 grams** of melatonin powder

This follows a ratio of:

* **1,000 mg melatonin per 5 mL Liposence**

This 20 mL batch should last roughly **3 weeks** at **180 mg/day**.

Daily use from that batch:

* draw **0.9 mL** per day with a 1ml needle-less syringe
* that 0.9 mL contains **180 mg melatonin**
* store in the fridge in a clean amber bottle away from light
* clean and dry the nee&#x64;_&#x6C;ess_ 1ml measuring syringe immediately after use

</details>

### Full-bottle logic - 180mg dosing, 222 doses

<details>

<summary>How to make 7 months worth at 180mg per day</summary>

A full **200 mL** Liposence bottle gives:

* 200 mL ÷ 5 mL = 40 servings
* 1,000 mg × 40 = 40,000 mg melatonin total

At **180 mg/day**, that works out to:

* 40,000 mg ÷ 180 mg/day ≈ 222 days

That is about **7 months and 12 days**.

Many people may prefer smaller batches, such as one 5 mL mix at a time, especially if they want to use the same Liposence bottle for creating other DIY liposomal formulas too.

</details>
