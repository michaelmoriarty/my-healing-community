# Anticancer Mechanisms

Usnic acid appears to act across several cancer hallmarks at once.

### Cell-cycle arrest and apoptosis

UA has induced cell-cycle arrest at G0/G1, S, and G2/M in multiple models.

It also promotes apoptosis, often through mitochondrial stress, ROS generation, lower mitochondrial membrane potential, higher Bax, and lower Bcl-2.

### Pathway suppression

Preclinical work suggests downregulation of:

* Wnt
* MAPK
* PI3K/Akt/mTOR
* STAT3
* NF-κB

These pathways matter because they support cancer-cell growth, survival, adaptation, and resistance.

### Anti-angiogenic activity

UA has shown anti-angiogenic activity through suppression of VEGFR2-related signalling, including downstream MEK/ERK and Akt/p70S6K effects in some models.

### EMT, invasion, and metastasis

UA has reduced EMT-linked markers such as Snail, Twist, and N-cadherin.

It has also been linked to reduced motility and invasion.

### Immune-evasion relevance

Preclinical studies suggest:

* lower PD-L1 expression
* IDO1 inhibition

That makes UA relevant to tumour–immune interaction, not just direct tumour-cell pressure.

### Inflammatory signalling

UA has been linked to lower COX-2, hyaluronidase, TNF-α, IL-6, NO, and PGE2 in relevant models.

That supports its relevance in tumour-promoting inflammatory environments.

### Metabolic disruption

UA acts as a mitochondrial uncoupler.

That can reduce ATP production, increase energy stress, and impair both oxidative phosphorylation and glycolysis.

### Autophagy modulation

UA can induce autophagy.

That may be protective in some settings and sensitising in others.

This is one reason UA combination logic can become highly context-dependent.

### 14-3-3 protein relevance

One of the most distinctive mechanistic angles is UA’s reported interaction with 14-3-3 proteins.

These proteins regulate survival, signalling, metabolism, and invasion. That gives UA a more unusual mechanistic profile than many general phytochemicals.

### Bottom line

UA is best understood as a multi-pressure compound rather than a single-target agent.

That is its strength.

It is also why oversimplified claims about it tend to miss the real story.
