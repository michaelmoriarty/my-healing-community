# Colorectal Cancer

## Colorectal Cancer

### Why this cancer is a plausible fit

Colorectal cancer frequently overexpresses `STAT3`, BCL-2 family proteins, `PKM2`-driven aerobic glycolysis, and `Wnt/β-catenin`. Apigenin modulates all of these.

Both sporadic and colitis-associated colorectal cancer models have been studied. This is one of the strongest settings in the apigenin literature.

### Cell data

Apigenin has been shown to:

* arrest colorectal cancer cells in `G2/M` across multiple cell lines, including `SW480`, `HT-29`, `Caco-2`, `HCT116`, `COLO320`, and `DLD-1`
* induce apoptosis in a caspase-dependent and dose-dependent manner
* downregulate `BCL-xL` and `MCL-1` through `STAT3` suppression in colon cancer cells
* inhibit `PKM2`-regulated glycolysis, reducing proliferation and metabolic advantage
* suppress `Wnt/β-catenin` signalling and reduce stemness-related features
* reverse cisplatin resistance in resistant `HCT116` cells, triggering autophagy and programmed cell death

In one model, blocking autophagy further enhanced apigenin-induced apoptosis. That suggests autophagy can act as a temporary survival buffer in this setting.

### Animal data

In colitis-associated mouse models, apigenin reduced `STAT3` and `NF-κB` phosphorylation in tumour tissue. Meta-analysis of animal studies also found significant reductions in tumour volume and tumour weight across studied models that included colorectal cancer.

In cisplatin-resistant colon xenograft models, apigenin inhibited tumour growth in vivo.

### Human data

No direct human colorectal cancer trials exist.

A registered human study (`NCT03526081`) examined apigenin pharmacokinetics in healthy volunteers, but it was not a cancer outcome study.

### Clinical positioning

Colorectal cancer is one of the clearest settings in apigenin’s preclinical oncology literature.

The combination of glycolytic interference, BCL-2 family modulation, cisplatin sensitisation, and in vivo tumour suppression gives colorectal cancer one of the strongest overall cases. No human trial data exist.
