# Colorectal Cancer

EGCG is the main green tea catechin, and colorectal cancer is one of the most biologically relevant targets because oral EGCG achieves high local exposure in the gastrointestinal tract.

### Overview

The colorectal literature includes chemoprevention, adenoma recurrence work, inflammatory-pathway suppression, and adjunctive interest with 5-FU.

### Key human data

* Clinical work has suggested benefit in recurrent adenoma prevention
* Large-cohort epidemiology links green tea intake with lower colorectal cancer risk in several populations
* Local colonic exposure is one reason this area is considered especially relevant

### Key preclinical data

* EGCG inhibits NF-κB and COX-2 in colorectal cancer models
* Induces apoptosis and cell-cycle arrest
* Reduces cancer stem-cell behaviour and may improve 5-FU sensitivity
* Suppresses Wnt/β-catenin-linked proliferation and metastasis biology

### Clinical positioning

This is one of the more credible settings for EGCG because the compound may matter both systemically and locally in colonic tissue.
