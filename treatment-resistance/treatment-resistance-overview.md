### Immunotherapy Treatment

When researchers asked whether there were metabolic signals in the blood and gut that tell us who is more likely to benefit from immunotherapy, histidine stood out as one of the strongest signals.

#### What was learned

* Higher baseline blood histidine was linked to longer progression-free survival and longer overall survival in several cohorts.
* The gut microbiome mattered. In eubiosis, histidine tracked with more favourable response patterns. In dysbiosis, it was more often converted into metabolites such as imidazole propionate, which tracked with worse outcomes.
* Mouse work suggested histidine may do more than mark response. It may also support CD8 T-cell fitness and help immunotherapy work better.

For the full summary, see [Diet and Gut Health May Shape Immunotherapy Response](../breast-cancer/er-positive-her2-negative/endocrine-therapy-resistance-and-dormancy/diet-and-gut-health-may-shape-immunotherapy-response.md).
