# Treatment Resistance Overview

This section gathers treatment-resistance topics that cut across cancer types, regimens, and biomarkers.

It exists for the questions that sit underneath progression.

Why did treatment stop working?

What survival route did the cancer use?

Was the problem tumour biology, drug exposure, dormancy, senescence, metabolism, or some combination of them?

### How to use this section

1. Start with the page that best matches the resistance question.
2. Use related hubs when the answer depends on biomarkers or drug metabolism.
3. Use cancer-type sections when the issue is clearly disease-specific.
4. Use the cross-cancer hubs first when the same biology may apply across several tumour types.

***

### What this section helps answer

Use this section when the real question is not only _what happened_, but _how the cancer adapted_.

That includes:

* dormancy and residual disease
* therapy-induced senescence and senolytic follow-up
* autophagy as a survival route under treatment pressure
* supplement interactions that may alter drug exposure
* biomarker-defined resistance logic
* progression patterns that do not fit a simple one-drug explanation
