# Inflammation, Stress and Dormancy Escape

Dormant cells do not wake up at random.

Inflammation, tissue injury, and stress signalling can help push them out of dormancy.

### Why this matters

This biology helps explain why recurrence risk can shift after:

* infection
* surgery
* major tissue injury
* persistent inflammatory disease
* severe or chronic stress

These are not the only triggers.

They are some of the most plausible and most discussed.

### Key inflammatory drivers

The dormancy literature repeatedly highlights:

* **NETs**
* **S100A8/A9**
* inflammatory cytokines
* myeloid-derived suppressor cells
* tissue-remodelling signals in the local niche

These signals can change extracellular matrix structure, immune tone, and cell-adhesion behaviour in ways that favour outgrowth.

### Stress signalling also matters

Adrenergic stress signals are not just emotional concepts.

They are real tumour-biology inputs.

Signals involving **epinephrine**, **norepinephrine**, and related stress pathways can support:

* immune suppression
* pro-metastatic signalling
* myeloid-cell recruitment
* escape from dormancy in some models

### The perioperative window

The period around surgery is one of the clearest real-world examples.

Pain, anaesthesia, tissue injury, blood loss, and inflammatory repair signals all converge there.

For broader perioperative context, see [Surgery Support; Before and After Care](../../../surgery-support-before-and-after-care/).

### Practical interpretation

Stress management is not just soft support.

Inflammation control is not just comfort care.

Both can be part of a rational dormancy-maintenance strategy.

That does not make them replacements for oncology treatment.

It makes them biologically relevant adjuncts.

### Examples people may want to discuss with their team

* peri-treatment (surgery, radiation, chemo) inflammation control
* infection prevention and early treatment
* pain control that reduces unnecessary stress load without dampening the immune response
* whether **beta-blockade** is appropriate in selected settings
* how to reduce avoidable inflammatory spikes around procedures

### Bottom line

Dormancy escape is strongly shaped by the environment around the cell.

Inflammation and stress are two of the most important environmental signals to take seriously.
