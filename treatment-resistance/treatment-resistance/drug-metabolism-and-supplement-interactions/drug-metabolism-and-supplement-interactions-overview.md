# Drug Metabolism & Supplement Interactions Overview

This section is the shared home for drug-metabolism and supplement-interaction pages that cut across multiple compounds.

### How to use this section

Keep **compound-specific interaction notes** inside each compound's own pharmacokinetics or safety page.

Use this section for the **shared framework** that would otherwise be repeated many times, such as:

* CYP3A4
* CYP2C9
* CYP1A2
* P-glycoprotein
* BCRP
* inducer vs inhibitor logic
* treatment-specific interaction pages

### Why it is organised this way

* compound pages stay practical and focused
* shared enzyme explanations only need to be written once
* treatment pages can compare several supplements side by side
* future updates become easier as new compounds are added

### Where it connects to treatment-specific topics

Some pages built from this shared interaction framework may live inside treatment-specific subtopics when that creates a better reading flow.

The HER2CLIMB pages are one example of that approach.
